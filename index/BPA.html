BPA

What is BPA?

BPA stands for Business Professionals of America. It is something that you must participate in when you join this lab. BPA hosts dozens of different contests that are all used to test your skills in different areas. Some time in the first you are required to sign up to compete in at least one contest with a max of three contests. Mr.Roeth will help you prep for whatever contest you may choose to participate in. Click HERE for a list of the contests.

What is the benefit of making it far?

BPA starts at the regional level with us competing against other nearby schools. To make it to states which is the next level You need to place in the top 8 of whatever contest you participate in. The difficulty increase from regionals to states are very noticable so you'll spend a good chunk of time preparing for the contest before hand. From states you need to place top 8 to go to nationals which is pretty close to if not an all expense paid trip to different state to compete again for the final time that year. For the school year of 2025-2026 we are going to Nashville Tennessee for nationals.

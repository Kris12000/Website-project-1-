About Our Lab

Here is everything you will be learning in the programming class during your two years at ECC

1st Semester of Junior year

You will learn Python, Excel and C#. Also, you'll have a chance to compete at the regional level and get certified in excel.

2nd Semester of Junior year

You will learn HTML, CSS, and javascript. Also, if you placed in states you will be able to go to nationals.

1st semester of Senior Year

You will be learning Java, object-oriented programming, C# and unity. Also, You will be getting deeper into excel by learning pivot table pivot charts and compound formulas

2nd semester of Senior Year

You will be learning mobile and computer appilcations on a grander scale. Also, more mobile applications of websites.
